Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 10, 2026

Last Updated: October 10, 2026

Mutimer ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Mutimer handles your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information Collection and Storage

Mutimer is designed from the ground up as a privacy-first, offline-capable application.

  • Timetables and Schedules: All class periods, subjects, schedules, special day overrides, and ringer mode preferences are stored locally on your device using encrypted Android Room database architecture and DataStore preferences. We do not transmit, back up, or store your timetable data on any external server or cloud platform.
  • No Personally Identifiable Information: We do not collect, request, record, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or contacts.

2. Device Permissions and Sensor Usage

To provide precise Do Not Disturb (DND) automation and intuitive interface features, Mutimer requests the following device permissions and hardware access:

  • Do Not Disturb Access (ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY): Used to automatically adjust your device's ringer mode (Vibrate, Silent, or Normal) during scheduled class periods and breaks according to your preferences.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to schedule precise system alarms so that ringer mode adjustments occur at the scheduled start and end times of your classes.
  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to display essential notifications, automation status updates, and post-class overtime alerts.
  • Boot Receiver (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used to reschedule timetable alarms after your device reboots.
  • Motion Sensors (Accelerometer and Gyroscope): Used exclusively on-device to detect device tilt and roll for the dynamic "Auto" floating action button positioning feature. Sensor data is processed locally in real time and is not recorded, saved, or transmitted off your device.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Mutimer utilizes third-party software development tools and services to support app security and stability.

  • Google Play Services and Firebase App Check: Used for app integrity verification, protection against tampering, and other app functionality as implemented. These services may process certain device or diagnostic information, such as Android version information and app-integrity attestation tokens, subject to the applicable Google privacy terms. Read the Google Privacy Policy.
  • No Advertising or Commercial Tracking: Mutimer does not include third-party advertisements or behavioral advertising analytics, and does not integrate with data brokers or marketing platforms.

4. Data Sharing, Sale, and Transfer

Mutimer does not upload your timetable data to external servers. We do not sell, trade, rent, or lease your personal information.

Third-party services integrated into the App may process limited technical information as described in Section 3 and under their own applicable privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Mutimer is intended for a general student audience, including high school, college, and university students. The App does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate corrective action.

6. Your Data Control and Deletion

You retain control over the timetable information stored locally on your device.

  • You can create, modify, or delete timetable entries directly within the App.
  • Clearing the App's data or uninstalling Mutimer through Android System Settings removes locally stored schedules, settings, and database files, subject to Android system behavior and any separate backups managed by you or your device.

7. AI-Generated Timetable Disclaimer

Important Notice Mutimer is not responsible for errors, omissions, or inaccuracies in timetables generated by generative AI tools or entered by users. Always verify your timetable, class timings, and special day schedules before enabling automation.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, our practices, or applicable legal requirements. Updates will be published within the App or on its official store listing page, along with a revised "Last Updated" date.
