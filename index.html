Privacy Policy for Mutimer Effective Date: October 10, 2026 Last Updated: October 10, 2026 Mutimer ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Mutimer handles your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). 1. Information Collection and Storage Mutimer is designed from the ground up as a privacy-first, offline-capable application. • Timetables & Schedules: All class periods, subjects, schedules, special day overrides, and ringer mode preferences are stored 100% locally on your device using encrypted Android Room database architecture and DataStore preferences. We do not transmit, back up, or store your timetable data on any external server or cloud platform. • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect, request, record, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or contacts. 2. Device Permissions & Sensor Usage To provide precise Do Not Disturb (DND) automation and intuitive interface features, Mutimer requests the following device permissions and hardware access: • Do Not Disturb Access (ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY): Used strictly to automatically toggle your device's ringer mode (Vibrate, Silent, Normal) during your scheduled class periods and breaks according to your preferences. • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to schedule precise system alarms so that ringer mode adjustments occur exact to the second when your classes begin and end. • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to display essential foreground system notifications, automation status updates, and post-class overtime alerts. • Boot Receiver (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used to automatically reschedule your timetable alarms after your device reboots. • Motion Sensors (Accelerometer & Gyroscope): Used exclusively on-device to detect device tilt and roll for the dynamic "Auto" floating action button positioning feature. Sensor telemetry is processed locally in real time and is never recorded, saved, or transmitted off your device. 3. Third-Party Services & Analytics Mutimer utilizes minimal, industry-standard Third-Party Software Development Kits (SDKs) solely to maintain application security and app stability: • Google Play Services & Firebase App Check: Used exclusively for app integrity, anti-tampering verification, and system font rendering. These security services may process basic device diagnostic metadata (such as Android version and safety attestation tokens) strictly in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). • No Advertising or Commercial Tracking: Mutimer contains no third-party advertisements, no behavioral tracking analytics, and zero integrations with data brokers or marketing platforms. 4. Data Sharing, Sale, and Transfer Because we do not collect or store your data on external servers, we do not sell, trade, rent, lease, or transfer any user data to third parties under any circumstances. 5. Children's Privacy Mutimer is intended for general student audiences, primarily high school, college, and university students. The App does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective action. 6. Your Data Control & Deletion You retain 100% ownership and control over your data: • You can create, modify, or permanently delete any timetable entry directly within the App. • Clearing app data or uninstalling Mutimer through Android System Settings immediately and permanently deletes all stored schedules, settings, and database files from your device. 7.
